Bienvenido a Cabañas SurVal / Küme Akun / Welcome

Agencia especializada en el arriendo y venta de cabañas en la Región de La Araucanía. Promovemos un servicio accesible, inclusivo y conectado con el territorio.

Mari mari pu che. Tüfati ruka küme meli witran mapu mew. Re küme ruka amuleyiñ kom che gealu.

Specialized agency for cabin rentals and sales in La Araucanía Region. We provide accessible, inclusive, and territory-connected services.

Küme ruka mamüll mew deumañmangeyei. Niey mawida, lafken witrun, küme kütral, kay kay meli che müleyiñ.

Rustic wooden cabin next to native forest with lake view. Equipped with deck, double-pane windows, wood stove, and capacity for 6 guests.
